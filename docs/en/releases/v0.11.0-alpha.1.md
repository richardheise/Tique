# Tique 0.11.0 — movements and batch review

Build verified on 2026-10-31. Physical evaluation on the phone is still pending.

Update over the existing installation; do not uninstall. Package, signature, databases, and keys must be preserved. The interface now has two tabs: **Movements** and **Analysis**; **Data and capture** is in the gear menu. Importing and manual entry remain optional and independent of notification permission.

## Everyday use

Receiving CSV/OFX prepares candidates; it does not confirm income or spending. Review amounts, dates, and descriptions, select the items you want, and explicitly choose the type and classification for the group. Confirm only ordinary income or expenses. Own-account transfers, withdrawals, deposits, bill payments, reversals, and unsupported refunds remain pending; the file's sign does not decide this for you.

Possible duplicates/links require individual review. A link associates evidence with an existing record without creating another entry. Legitimate identical purchases remain distinct. Repeating confirmation or re-importing does not authorize duplication.

After confirmation, use **View records period** and choose the matching month. Analysis shows confirmed items only, including uncategorized ones. Pending items are excluded from totals, and no chart guarantees that the statement is complete or that the result equals the bank balance.

Batch selection is temporary and cleared when the app goes to the background for privacy. Candidates, saved corrections, and records remain in encrypted storage; absence of a selection does not delete data. Financial backups continue to include confirmed records and the catalog, without staging or raw data.

No new manual test run was requested: use the app normally and report difficulties. Reusable rules, reclassification of confirmed entries, further comparisons, and financial support for savings spaces/cards remain in the backlog.
