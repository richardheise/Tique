# Tique 0.10.0 — daily use

Three screens: **Sources**, **Movements**, and **Analysis**. The app tracks recorded movements; it does not require matching the bank balance. Black, amber, cyan, and off-white, short navigation, forms, and settings in panels.

Update **over the existing installation; do not uninstall**. Version 0.10.0/code 13, with package and certificate preserved. Migrations preserve data, keys, and classifications; no diagnostic consent is promoted automatically.

## Everyday use

- In **Sources**, grant Android access and tap **Activate** to consent to daily receipt of Nubank notifications. You can pause it there. You do not need to import a statement or enter a balance to get started.
- In **Movements → To review**, check what arrived. Amount, type, and date must be correct before confirming. Category can remain **Uncategorized**. **+** records anything missing manually. **Recorded** offers search, filters, and details by day.
- An existing record can receive another piece of evidence through **Link**, after confirmation. Equal amounts do not prove duplication. Do not confirm the same movement again as income/expense.
- In **Analysis**, follow the result and charts for recorded items in the month. Tap a category/bar to view movements. Pending items are excluded from totals; the result does not represent the bank's available balance.
- The gear groups categories, backup, and recovery. Automatic internal copies are the default. A password-protected portable backup is optional; it includes confirmed records and the catalog, but not pending items/raw data, and can survive uninstalling if saved outside the app. Internal copies do not.

No new manual test run was requested. Use the app for a few days and note what gets in the way, takes too long, or is unclear. Development checks continue to run here.

## Optional statement: export to Tique

Export from Nubank and download the file to your phone. Open/share the account **CSV or OFX** with **Tique**, or use **Sources → Import statement**. For a shared file, confirm **Receive file**. The app prepares candidates locally; importing does not post transactions or enable notifications. The Open Nubank button is only a shortcut to the banking app; it does not read screens or retrieve statements automatically.

Initially accepted subset: UTF-8 (optional BOM), CSV with recognizable date/amount/description columns and optional identifier; comma or semicolon; YYYY-MM-DD or DD/MM/YYYY dates and exact cents. OFX 1/2 for BRL accounts, with valid dates/amounts/FITIDs. Up to 2 MiB/2,000 lines. PDF, card files, and other encodings/layouts may be rejected as a whole, without partial posting. No real sample was examined to claim compatibility with your Nubank file.

Importing the same file again does not create duplicate candidates. An identified account plus FITID can recognize overlapping OFX entries; without a reliable account/ID, overlapping periods require review and an explicit link. Conflicting content for a statement identifier does not silently replace its entry. The sign suggests inflow/outflow but does not confirm the type: transfers, deposits/withdrawals, and bill payments are not ordinary income/consumption. Unsupported types must remain under review.

## Protection and limits

Native daily capture with consent, SQLCipher persistence before parsing, serial processing independent of JavaScript, package/signature filtering, and recovery of interrupted parsing. The first unlock after restart is required; force-stop, revocation, Android/battery policies, and missing notifications still limit collection. Complete capture and automatic postings are not promised.

Daily originals expire after 30 days when the core runs; candidates/corrections persist. When an original expires, the provisional description copied from the notification is also removed; approved descriptions and imported movements remain. Older diagnostics retain their seven-day limit. Source details are available on demand. The BRL parser remains generic; Nubank support is not declared validated.

Schema 2→3 and financial backup v2 with links; restore accepts v1. There is no separate TypeScript bank or ledger for real data. No new dependencies, backend, telemetry, or INTERNET permission in the APK. Opening the database/enabling capture uses internal checks and does not require the development runner. Statements pass through memory and encrypted staging, with no plaintext temporary file or third-party upload. [Decision and consequences](https://github.com/richardheise/Tique/blob/v0.10.0-alpha.1/docs/adr/0012-uso-diario-e-entrada-de-extratos.md).
