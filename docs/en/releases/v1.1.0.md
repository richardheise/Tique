# Draft 1.1 — unpublished

Replaced by [1.1.1](v1.1.1.md) to preserve the place tag in batch confirmations as well. This draft's tag and attachments remain immutable.

Update over the current version; do not uninstall.

- Void purchases, payments, and savings-space movements entered by mistake. History and the reason are preserved; the app recalculates its records. This does not move money in the bank.
- If another record depends on the operation, voiding is blocked with an explanation. For example, to void a purchase that has already been paid, review its payment first. After voiding, use Record correction to enter the correct movement.
- During review, fill in **What is the place?** and tap **Remember this place**. The place tag will be created/reused along with the category you choose. User-provided example: BLUMENAUENSE as the place and Comida (Food) as the category.
- In Rules, add other names used by the same place. Review the preview before applying changes to history. Ambiguous names require review.
- Notifications get type and date suggestions when the text contains explicit clues. Pix remains only a payment method; it does not imply income or expense. Check the fields before confirming. Under View source, you can inspect the clues and received text.

There is no automatic posting or complete Nubank recognition. Rules and clues reduce manual entry; notifications may be missing or lack enough date/type information. No real samples were used to certify bank formats. Financial data and files are not sent to third parties.

Financial backup v5, with support for reading earlier formats; schema 6 preserves existing data, keys, and IDs. Do not use an older APK to open updated data. Originals retain their limited retention period; confirmed transactions are not deleted.
