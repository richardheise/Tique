# Tique v0.8.0-alpha.2 — first real financial ledger

One Nubank account in BRL, manual income/expenses, confirmation of reviewed notifications, permanent history, balance, and monthly summary by category. Categories and tags are preserved and included in financial backup. The app now opens to the ledger; synthetic tools, diagnostics, and checks are in **Capture tools and checks**.

**Install over the existing app; do not uninstall.** APK 0.8.0/versionCode 11, same signature and package, no INTERNET, offline bundle. Existing catalog migrates to the encrypted ledger while keeping key and IDs. Expo Go demo remains exclusively synthetic.

## What changes

- Configure opening date and balance before movements you will enter. Do not count operations already included in that balance again. Opening is not income and does not include savings pockets or credit limit.
- Enter account inflows/outflows or use **Interpret original → Confirm in financial ledger**. Confirmation requires nature Income/Expense, amount, date, and checking that the movement affects available account funds. Publication is not automatically used as effective date.
- Confirming/reprocessing again does not duplicate. Two identical purchases with different commands remain separate. Category, tags, and approved fields survive diagnostics cleanup/expiry.
- **Void entry** requires a reason, preserves history, and removes its effect from totals. It does not reverse a bank transaction or operate in the bank. To correct amount/nature, void and enter the correct one.
- **Check balance** compares a reported balance with the calculated balance through the end of the date. It shows the difference without creating an adjustment or income/expense. Importing files exported from Nubank is for the next increment, with preview and reconciliation; no real format was assumed here.
- Automatic local copies include ledger and catalog: up to three, at most one per 24 hours of use with changes, starting from opening setup. **Advanced options** provides password-protected portable backup, recovery, previous vaults, and recoverable ledger reset. No diagnostic raw data or key. Portable backup saved outside the app can restore after uninstall; private copies do not survive uninstall.

## On-phone procedure

Start with fictitious amounts to check the flow; they will remain in the test ledger, which can be preserved as the previous vault when restarting.

1. Install over 0.7.1 without uninstalling. Check that existing categories and tags remain.
2. Open **Capture tools and checks → Advanced settings → Run isolated native checks**. Expected: **72 passed, zero failures, zero not verified**. The battery uses invented data and isolated storage; it does not post to your ledger. It pauses capture. Report counters/codes only, without financial data.
3. Return to the ledger and set opening to **R$ 1,000.00**, dated today. Enter fictitious income of **R$ 200.00** and expense of **R$ 50.00**, category Food, and one test tag. Expected: balance **R$ 1,150.00**, income **R$ 200.00**, expenses/Food **R$ 50.00**.
4. Exit and reopen the app: balance, history, and classification should remain. Void the expense with reason “Test.” Expected: balance **R$ 1,200.00**, expenses **R$ 0.00**, entry visible as voided; no extra R$ 50.00 income.
5. Enter two fictitious expenses of **R$ 10.00** with the same fields. Both should appear; balance **R$ 1,180.00** and expenses **R$ 20.00**. Rename the category/tag and reopen: links should show the new name.
6. Record a balance check of **R$ 1,190.00** for today. Expected: calculated **R$ 1,180.00**, difference **R$ 10.00**. Balance, income, and expenses do not change.
7. In **Advanced options**, check that local recovery is ready and at least the opening copy exists. It does not promise to contain every later same-day change. Export financial backup with a 12–128 character password, saving outside the app's private data. Do not share the file or password here.
8. After backup, rename a tag and leave one field filled in the form; enter a fictitious **R$ 5.00** expense (balance R$ 1,175.00). Try restoring with a wrong password: current ledger must remain. Restore with the correct password: balance **R$ 1,180.00**, void and classifications preserved, previous vault available. The form catalog should show names from the restored backup and clear selections from the previous vault. Switching to previous returns **R$ 1,175.00**; switching back returns **R$ 1,180.00**. Another restore is blocked while a previous vault exists. There is no merge.
9. To test capture linking, start the diagnostic session again with consent and wait for a normal-use notification that actually affects account balance. In **Interpret original**, check amount, nature, date, and account, then confirm in the ledger. Reopen/reprocess: there should be one entry. Do not treat credit purchase, bill payment, transfer, contribution, or reversal as income/expense. A bank operation is not required just to test.
10. If you decide to delete temporary diagnostic records, confirm financial history remains. The original will no longer be available; approved fields and minimal provenance remain in ledger/backup.
11. To leave fictitious data, choose which vault to keep, check portable backup, and resolve the previous vault through the interface. Then **Restart ledger** creates an empty ledger with the same catalog and preserves the current one as previous. Configure an opening balance consistent with the movements you will track; do not uninstall to clear the test. Restart requires space for the previous vault and does not silently delete records.

Restoring after uninstall is optional for this procedure; the app does not need to be erased. The battery includes key loss in isolated synthetic storage. A valid portable backup is required to recover outside the installation.

## Limits and validation

Capture remains optional diagnostics, with sessions up to 24 hours, limit of 200 observations, and seven-day retention. There is no lossless capture, validated Nubank parser, or automatic posting. Confirmed ledger is permanent. User-approved descriptions may contain personal data; they are not advertised as anonymized. Financial backup does not include the diagnostic raw-payload set. Synthetic file and financial backup are distinct formats.

This step supports only account income/expenses. Cards, bills, transfers, reversals, savings pockets, real import, and projection require their own increments. Calculated balance depends on reported movements; differences are not silently corrected. Limits: 10,000 movements (including opening and voids), 1,000 balance checks, and snapshot up to 8 MiB; records are not automatically deleted to fit. Monthly history paginates in groups of 50.

Verified locally: strict TypeScript and 30 TypeScript tests; 54 JVM tests; 70 Android scenarios compiled. Android JUnit was not run on this server; no device was connected.
