# Tique 1.0

Update over the existing installation; do not uninstall. Analysis is now the first screen, followed by Movements. The new Space Grotesk font works offline. Explanations about operation and security are in **Settings → About Tique**.

## What's new

- Change categories and tags on confirmed records, individually or in batches.
- Create, edit, and disable local classification rules; preview them before applying them to history.
- Enter savings spaces and their type, record deposits/withdrawals, and transfer between spaces without counting these as income or spending.
- Add a card, statement closing/due dates, purchases, and previous debt. Record partial or full payments without counting the bill as a new expense.
- Compare expenses with the same portion of the previous month and view uncategorized spending. Periods may be incomplete.

You do not need to enter a bank balance or import a statement to get started. Savings spaces and debts reflect what you enter; they are not retrieved automatically from the bank. For a card-purchase notification, record the purchase in the cards area and link the notification to the matching record to avoid confirming a second expense.

## Limits of this version

Card cycles use days 1–28 and should be checked against your statement. Installments, interest, automatic earnings, and arbitrary transfers between bank accounts are not yet available. Savings-space/card movements cannot be voided in this version; check the amount, date, and destination before recording. Ordinary income/expense retains the existing audited void operation. Do not simulate adjustments with false income/expense entries.

Backup v3 includes the new accounts, rules, and audit data; reading backups v1/v2 remains supported. Financial backups do not include original notification text or pending items. Keep a password-protected copy outside the app before changing phones; uninstalling removes data and internal copies.
