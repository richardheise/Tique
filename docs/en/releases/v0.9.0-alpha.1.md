# Tique 0.9.0 — dashboard and navigation

Applies approved visual concept 02: black `#080B10`, amber gold `#FFB020`, cyan `#9DECF4`, off-white `#F3EFE6`, straight shapes, and small corner radii. Implements interactive native charts and organizes flows into **Home**, **Movements**, **Review**, and **More**, with a central **+ New** button. No new dependency, remote chart library, or external service.

**Install over the existing app; do not uninstall.** Version 0.9.0/code 12, same signature and package. Database, keys, financial rules, and backup format remain as in 0.8.0. The native functional change is a query for already-confirmed IDs to remove those observations from the review queue; it does not delete records.

## How to use

- **Home:** current calculated account balance, dated check, income/expenses for the selected month, monthly result, categories, and five recent active movements.
- **Charts:** category share of expenses and income/expense comparison on the same scale. Tapping a bar opens matching movements. Categories are sorted by value, with an option to show all.
- **Movements:** search by description/category/tag, case- and accent-insensitive; income/expense and category filters. Voided items remain visible in history but are outside totals. Pages of 50 records.
- **Review:** observations not confirmed or discarded. Group summaries remain in advanced tools. Confirmation does not depend on the screen to preserve the link; the ledger query identifies already-confirmed items, including records later voided.
- **+ New:** choose income or expense and open the form. **More:** categories/tags, balance check, backup, and recovery. **More → Advanced options → Advanced: capture, permissions, and checks** opens technical tools; there, **Advanced settings** groups capture and runner.
- **Hide values:** masks summary numbers and charts and keeps the state when changing month or navigating. After backgrounding, the summary is hidden again. This is not a biometric lock and does not replace encryption; values remain available when opening history.

## Chart correctness

Balance is current and includes all ledger movements. Income, expenses, result, and charts refer to the selected month. They are not net worth, forecast, budget, or complete bank statement. Balance-check label always includes its date; differences do not create automatic adjustments.

The model validates cents and totals with BigInt. Sum of categories must equal expenses. Shares are calculated in 10,000 integer basis points; displayed percentages are rounded. Only visual width uses the basis-point ratio. No monetary calculation depends on floating point. Empty months show an empty state. Voided items are not in totals or recent movements; legitimate identical records remain distinct. Changing month hides the previous summary while the new one loads. Restoring/switching vaults clears old filters and forms.

## Test on the phone

1. Install without uninstalling; check previous data. Physical validation of 0.8.0 was not reported in this round; this version keeps the same core and [financial procedure](https://github.com/richardheise/Tique/blob/v0.8.0-alpha.2/docs/releases/v0.8.0-alpha.2.md).
2. Check the four bottom destinations and rectangular **+** button. It should offer Income/Expense. Enter fictitious data if still validating: opening R$ 1,000, income R$ 200, expense R$ 50 → balance R$ 1,150, monthly result R$ 150. Opening is excluded from income.
3. On Home, tap a category and check filtered history. Clear the filter, test All/Income/Expense, and search description or tag with/without accents. Void the fictitious expense with a reason: charts should remove its effect, and history should retain it as voided.
4. Switch to an empty month: current account balance stays; monthly totals are zero, with no invented charts. Return and check previous values.
5. Hide summary values, change month and navigate between tabs: they should remain hidden when returning. Background the app and return; tap Show values when desired. Check Android enlarged font, keyboard, and touch targets on your A36.
6. In Review, open an observation, review, and confirm in the ledger only if it really represents account income/expense. Close the candidate/refresh list: confirmed item leaves the queue. Reprocess or reopen the same observation in tools; it should not duplicate. Dismissing a candidate also removes it from the queue; its temporary records are not deleted by this filter.
7. In More, check categories, balance check, backup/restore, and vault switching. Forms and filters must follow the active vault. Do not uninstall to test cleanup. If you have not run this installation's battery, use the tools above: expected **72 passed, zero failures, zero not verified**.

Report results/codes, without financial data, original notifications, or passwords. Capture remains diagnostics, with consent and limits of 24 hours/200 observations/7 days; ledger confirmation is permanent. No automatic posting, guaranteed capture, or validated Nubank parser. File import and other financial types are for their own increments.
